How can we help?

Email avteria2@gmail.com for account help, listing questions, privacy requests, safety concerns or moderation appeals.

Include relevant listing or message details and the email used for your account. Never send a password or confirmation code.

Report abuse

In Avteria, open a listing and choose Listing safety → Report listing, or tap Report beside a received message. You can block a user from the listing or conversation menu. For immediate danger, contact local emergency services.

Delete an account without the app

Use the account-deletion form. If you cannot sign in, email us from your registered address with the subject “Avteria account deletion”. We will verify ownership before removing account data. See the Privacy policy for the data removed and retention limitations.

Forgot your password?

Use “Forgot password?” on the Avteria sign-in screen. If you no longer have access to the app or your email, contact support for help verifying ownership.
