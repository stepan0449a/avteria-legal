Community rules

A marketplace for real vehicles and respectful conversations

Be accurate

Offer only vehicles you are authorized to sell. Use genuine photos and honest descriptions. Disclose title status and known condition issues. Do not impersonate another person, a dealership, Avteria or a government agency.

Keep it safe

No scams, stolen vehicles, requests for verification codes, suspicious payment schemes, malicious links or attempts to collect private documents. Do not advertise weapons, drugs, sexual services or other unrelated or illegal goods.

Respect people

No harassment, bullying, threats, hate speech, discrimination, sexually explicit material, graphic violence, exploitation of children or encouragement of self-harm. No unsolicited advertising, repetitive messages or other spam.

Respect privacy and ownership

Do not post another person's address, identity documents, financial information or private messages without permission. Upload only images and text you have the right to share.

How moderation works

Every new or edited listing is held for human review of its text and photos. A configurable phrase filter also checks messages, but a text filter cannot detect all abuse. Moderators investigate reports, remove violating content and may suspend accounts. Approval of a listing does not verify a vehicle or seller.

Report and block

Use Listing safety > Report listing, Report beside a received message, or Conversation safety > Report user. Give clear details. Reports are not shown to the reported user. You can also block a user from a listing or conversation and manage blocks in Profile. Moderators can view reported messages when investigating.

Contact and appeal

Contact avteria2@gmail.com for safety concerns or appeals. Give the listing or message details, but never send a password or email confirmation code. For immediate danger, contact local emergency services. We review reports and take action according to their urgency and the evidence available.
