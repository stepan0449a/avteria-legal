Privacy policy

Who operates Avteria

Avteria is operated by Stepan Ivanov. Contact: avteria2@gmail.com. This policy covers the Avteria mobile app and its account-deletion page.

Data you provide

We process your email address, account identifier, display name and city; vehicle descriptions, prices, specifications and photographs; favorites and saved searches; messages; blocked-user choices; reports and moderation decisions; and your acceptance of our terms. Authentication is handled by Supabase. Your password is not displayed to sellers or moderators. When deleting an account, it is sent over HTTPS to our deletion service only to verify your identity and is not intentionally logged.

Why we use data

We use this information to provide accounts, display listings, deliver messages, synchronize saved items, prevent abuse, review reports and honor account-deletion requests. A draft and display preferences are stored on your device. We use email to send confirmation and password-recovery codes and respond to support requests.

Visibility and sharing

Your display name and city are visible to signed-in members. Approved listings and photographs can be viewed by other members. Photograph URLs are public and anyone with a URL may view or copy them. Pending listing photos also use public URLs, although pending listings are not placed in the catalog. Do not upload identity documents, personal addresses or other sensitive information. Your email is not published in a listing. Conversations are available to their participants; moderators can inspect a reported message. This is not end-to-end encrypted messaging. Moderators can review listings and reports. Blocking hides the conversation and listings within the app but cannot recall copies already made.

Service providers

Supabase supplies authentication, database, file storage and server functions. Resend delivers account emails and receives the recipient address and message contents. Service providers may process operational information such as IP addresses, timestamps and technical security logs. Our database project is hosted in the United States; providers may process other operational data in additional locations. We do not sell personal data. The current app includes no advertising or analytics SDK and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Permissions

You choose photos through your device's photo picker. The app does not request GPS location, contacts, microphone access or broad background photo access. City is entered by you. Network access is used to operate the service. Do not include precise location or other personal information in text or photos unless you intend to share it.

Retention and deletion

Active account data remains until it is removed or the account is deleted. Removing a listing in the app archives it; its database record and photos remain until account deletion. Messages remain with the conversation until account deletion or moderation. Reports remain available for moderation while the related accounts exist. Pending or rejected photos may remain stored with their listing.

Use Profile > Delete account to permanently remove your account and associated profile, listings, uploaded originals, favorites, searches, consent record, blocks, reports and conversations, including messages visible to the other participant. The local draft on the requesting device is erased when deletion starts. If a network error interrupts cleanup, repeat the deletion or contact support. Once confirmed, deletion of these active records normally completes during the request. For large photo libraries you may need to continue deletion more than once.

You can also use the Avteria account-deletion website or email avteria2@gmail.com from your registered address to request deletion without the app. We may verify ownership before acting. Do not send a password or verification code by email.

Provider security logs and backups follow the providers' configured retention schedules and may not disappear immediately. Cached public images, especially photos uploaded by older app versions, may remain until their caches expire. We cannot erase copies saved by other people. Local drafts on other devices and backups of those devices are controlled separately; remove the app or its data from those devices. Contact support for information about the current provider retention settings or an unresolved deletion request.

Your choices

You may edit your profile, remove listings and saved items, block users, report content, request access to or correction of your information, and request deletion. Contact avteria2@gmail.com for these requests or questions about processing. Rights and available remedies depend on where you live.

Adults only

Avteria is intended for people aged 18 and over. If you believe a child has provided data, contact support so we can investigate and remove the account when appropriate.

Changes

We may update this policy as the service changes. The version date above identifies this policy. Material changes will be reflected in the app and published policy.
